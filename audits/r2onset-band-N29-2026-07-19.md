## The topology finding (the recurring rig blocker, cracked)
The band stalled for ~15 dead fires on the **dual-connection capture blind**: with the pad `BT→PS5` AND
`USB→PC` simultaneously, the USB stream carries the silicon clock (offset 28 keeps ticking → `t0_read`
fresh) but NONE of the button/trigger content — `r2` reads flat 0 even for a held press, and the buzz isn't
felt. `host=EXCLUSIVE_USB` is MISLEADING here (it is inferred from poll-rate stability, not content).
- **Fix:** disconnect the pad's Bluetooth from the PS5; pad is `USB→PC` ONLY; play *through the Remote Play
  window* so RP relays input to the PS5. A **bridge restart** was required to pick up the reconfigured pad.
- **What cracked it:** a diagnostic that logs the raw drain R2 (`data[6]` → `raw_r2_at_fire`) alongside the
  session-loop `ds.state` R2. A held-R2 test then showed **drain R2 = 255 AND session-loop R2 = 255** →
  input live → the topology was fixed. (Before the restart both read 0.)
