# Two planes: QorTroller and Qoresence

QorTroller and Qoresence are **separate repos** on purpose.

- **QorTroller** (this repo) is the parent truth plane. It makes cryptographic claims and keeps them fail-closed.
- **Qoresence** is the observation plane. It couples HDMI and DualSense on one clock and does not claim humanity.

Compose them. Do not OR-merge optical activity into `poep_enabled`. Do not treat a local clip as an on-chain receipt.
