### Crux: is HID OUTPUT a residual tell silence does not close?

**Split the attack classes — do not conflate them.**

| Attack | Signal | Free arm? | Closed by silence? |
|--------|--------|-----------|--------------------|
| **A-PRE-TELL** (F-POEP-LIVE-1) | stdout / fixed pre-window | **Yes ~400ms** before force | **YES** — that is this tier |
| **A-REACTIVE** (onset-coupled) | HID OUT force report / USB write | **No** — signal ≈ t_challenge (±1 USB frame) | **NO** — and should not claim to |

**Answer:** Watching the HID **OUTPUT** report (R2 force command) is a residual for *reactive* bots, not for the free-arm tell. Host issues the force → any co-resident process can see the write at **true challenge time**. That is **onset-coupling to the real stimulus**, equivalent (minus ~1ms) to feeling the actuator. Silence-on-stdout does **not** close A-REACTIVE; it was never the claim of P-LIVE-0.

**Is on-host onset-coupling fundamentally uncloseable?**  
**Yes, for pure host-timed proofs:** if the host both commands force and scores IMU, a host-side watcher always gets t_challenge. Closing A-REACTIVE as a *protocol* property requires something the host cannot pre-see or forge:

1. **Controller-firmware force timestamp / SE attestation** (best long-term), or  
2. **Actuator↔IMU physics that macros can’t fake** (waveform + Stage-A — already the flip gate), or  
3. **External witness** of the force event (camera/optical — Surface 4 class; out of scope here).

**For THIS tier:** firmware-side force timing is **deferrable**. The bankable win is defeating A-PRE-TELL with a demonstrated FAR collapse. Honest claim language: *“unpredictable to pre-arm macros; still not anti-reactive-host.”*

### 3. CATCH TRIALS acceptance bar

| Parameter | Bar |
|-----------|-----|
| **Go : no-go ratio** | **4:1** (20% catch) — enough signal, low human fatigue |
| **Human false-alarm (FA) budget** | **≤ 5%** of no-go trials marked “bot” (twitchy grip / tremor must not fail) |
| **Always-fire bot catch rate** | **≥ 90%** of no-go trials correctly FAIL the bot |
| **Tell-triggered bot** | On silent path: catch trials catch residual always-fire; go trials still require in-band post-challenge |

**How a no-go FAILs a bot without unfairly failing a human:**
- No-go = **no force** (or force=0 / no write), still full random arm delay  
- **FAIL bot:** IMU peak ≥ floor **or** fabricated “response” commitment without a real challenge event  
- **PASS human:** no above-threshold IMU event in the post-window (relaxed grip)  
- Twitchy human: require **joint** condition: peak ≥ floor **AND** response claims a go-challenge nonce that was never issued / no-go flag set — do **not** fail on low-level tremor alone; raise no-go threshold or require R2 onset coupling that only exists on go trials  

Optional soft rule: 2-of-3 consecutive no-go spikes → session fail (reduces single-twitch FA).
