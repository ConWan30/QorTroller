## Footguns (the hammer)

| # | Claim | Status | Evidence |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| **1** | Single SHA-256 then KMS `DIGEST` | **CLOSED** | `sign()` does `hashlib.sha256(body).digest()` once → port; fake uses `ECDSA(Prehashed(SHA256()))` = AWS DIGEST semantics; `test_body_is_hashed_exactly_once` asserts exact 32B digest ≠ body ≠ double-hash; `test_a_double_hashed_signature_would_fail_verify` + real `verify_cert_signature` prove the contract is load-bearing |
| **2** | Wire round-trip DER→raw 64B + SPKI→65B `0x04‖X‖Y` | **CLOSED** | Converters pure; verify against real `verify_cert_signature` (same path software uses); wrong-curve (secp256k1) fails closed at `setup()`; mirrors `SoftwareIdentityBackend.sign` `r.to_bytes(32)+s.to_bytes(32)` |

**Fake fidelity:** Real KMS `MessageType=DIGEST` + `ECDSA_SHA_256` = prehash, no second hash. Local `Prehashed(SHA256())` is the correct stand-in. The fake cannot hide a double-hash bug — the digest-byte assert would fail first, and end-to-end verify would fail second.

**Leading-zero r/s test this commit?** **No.** `int.to_bytes(32,"big")` zero-pads; verify uses `int.from_bytes` (same as software). Spot-check: 3/200 random KMS-path sigs had leading-zero r/s bytes; all verified. Not a ship gate.
