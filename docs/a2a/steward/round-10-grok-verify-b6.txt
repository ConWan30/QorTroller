## Findings (severity-first)

### F1 — **DESIGN / HIGH (answer to #5)** — inversion is real as **timeliness**, not as **suppression**
**Q:** Can force-CLOSED disable HIGH oversight when it matters?

| Layer | What happens if adversary denies presence |
|--------|-------------------------------------------|
| Detection / draft emission | Still runs — stewards still produce findings |
| Disposition | HIGH/MED → `BACKLOG`; LOW stays `ACTIVE` |
| Acting / spend | Still impossible (read-only, default-OFF, 0 IOTX) |

**Interpretation (load-bearing):**  
PGSW does **not** gate *detection*. It gates **ACTIVE disposition of HIGH-severity agentic reach** on operator liveness. That is human-in-the-loop for *when* HIGH drafts are “live,” not a kill-switch on scanning.

**Force-CLOSED attacker gains:** delay of HIGH ACTIVE surface (no present operator to review/act anyway).  
**Force-CLOSED attacker does *not* gain (if consumers honor semantics):** silent delete of CEILING_OVERCLAIM / other HIGH drafts.

**Footgun that *would* make inversion lethal:** a downstream that treats `BACKLOG` as drop → presence-DoS becomes full HIGH censorship. That is **outside this module**; this commit must pin the consumer contract: **BACKLOG is durable, queryable, and surfaces on reopen — never silent-drop.**

**Is the design right?**  
Yes for draft-only stewards: HIGH without a present operator is lower-value noise; parking is coherent. Complementary path already exists: **LOW stays ACTIVE** when closed (e.g. PCRA `UNBANKED_BUILD`). Anything that must fire *regardless of presence* must not be modeled only as HIGH+ACTIVE.

**Not a logic bug. Document as load-bearing honesty rail before arc close.**
