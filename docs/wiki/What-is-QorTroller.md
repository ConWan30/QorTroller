# What is QorTroller

QorTroller is the reference implementation of **Verifiable Autonomous Physical Intelligence (V.A.P.I.)**.

Gamers and their controllers produce data. Those same gamers keep cryptographic agency over that data. The protocol binds controller physics to a 228-byte Proof of Autonomous Cognition (PoAC) record and exposes eligibility as a public on-chain view call.

It is **not** a kernel anti-cheat and **not** the Qoresence observation console. Qoresence watches HDMI and HID on one clock. QorTroller is where receipts, consent, and eligibility live.
